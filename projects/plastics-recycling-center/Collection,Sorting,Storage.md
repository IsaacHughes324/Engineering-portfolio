Collection:

Large wheeled container to carry any recyclables.
Path through phase 2 and the cafeteria.
Collect from teachers rooms.


Sorting:

Sort by hand on the central table in the workshop, make sure to clean table of everything afterwards.
Sort by PET, HDPE, and pieces that need to be cut.
The unsorted needs to be sorted into the sorted PET, sorted HDPE, or Unsorted/to large.
Large peces need to be cut into smaller pices to be sorted.


Storage:

105" by 50" floor space for storage.
Multiple boxes that can be stacked.
Wood box/plastic box/milk crate/cardboard box.
Storage split into 4 sections for PET, HDPE, Unsorted, and to Large.


Safety:

PPE:
Foot protection-closed toed shoes.
Hand protection-gloves.
Eye protection.
Masks when shreding.
Ear protection.
Long sleaves.
Long pants.

Procegers:
Turn off and lockdown all machines and storage boxes.
Keep pathways and walkways clear of debris and trash.
Always clean and tidy up everything after use.
Spill kit on standby for spils.
First aid kit for emergincys.
Crate height limit is 3 high.
Be smart around cart, only use it for its intended use.
